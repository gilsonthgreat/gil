01 · OverviewWho He Is

A dusk-keeper who would rather understand a thing than judge it.

Rhada Evergarden is a seventeen-year-old dusk-keeper from Evergarden, the world of even days, where night and day always last the same length.

He belongs to the Riverloom clan, a people of weavers, growers and star-readers who keep their history by heart.

True neutral to the bone, he watches before he acts, weighs every side and rarely takes one, and stays loyal to the way things are rather than to any cause. It is not coldness; he simply believes most things find their level if they are given room, and he is very good at giving them room.

04 · PersonalityHis Nature

Calm, watchful and dry; kind without being a pushover.

Calm, watchful and dry, Rhada is kind without being a pushover and honest without being harsh. His patience can read as distance until you notice he remembers every word you said. He is warm in small, practical ways: a hand on the rope, a seat left open, a question asked twice so you can answer it properly.

Traits

  1. Watchful

    He listens to the whole of a thing before he answers any part of it. He notices who is tired, which way the wind is turning and what nobody is saying.

  2. Even-tempered

    Hard to anger and hard to flatter; praise and complaint get roughly the same nod. When a room gets loud, he gets quieter, and people tend to follow.

  3. Fair, not soft

    He will tell a friend they are wrong in front of the person they wronged, then help them both carry their things home.

  4. Unhurried

    He does not rush, and he will not be rushed into choosing. "Wait" is the word he uses most, sometimes a little too often.

  5. Accepting

    He would rather understand how something works than decide whether it should. His loyalty is to what is, not to what anyone wishes it were.

  6. Quietly funny

    His humour is dry, late and delivered with a straight face. Friends say you have to watch his eyes to know he is teasing.

05 · AlignmentTrue Neutral

The one fact nobody had to make up: he is true neutral, all the way down.

What it means for him

True neutral, and this file would underline it if it underlined anything. Rhada does not see the world as a contest between good and evil; he sees something closer to a loom, where every thread pulls against another and the cloth only holds when the tension is even.

He steps in when something tips too far, like a bully, a hoarder or a fire left burning, and steps back once it settles. People on opposite sides of an argument both trust him, and both are sometimes annoyed by him.

06 · HomeworldEvergarden

The world of even days

01 · Gold moon 02 · Evergarden 03 · Pale moon

A warm, slow-turning world that spins perfectly upright, so day and night always last the same length and there are no true seasons. Its gardens never stop growing.

Grass terraces and dark fruit trees step down to wide, quiet rivers, with red-clay mesas beyond. Its people like to say the planet taught them balance before they had a word for it.

07 · HistoryBefore

From the even hour he was born in to the watch he keeps now.

I

Chapter IBorn on the line

Rhada was born at the even hour, the exact moment Evergarden's day tips from light into dark. The Riverloom clan took it as a good sign: a child born on the line, they say, spends his life standing on it. The spoken record of his birth is two lines long and notes nothing unusual, which the clan considers a good beginning too.

II

Chapter IIBy the river

He grew up by a wide, quiet river below the grass terraces, slow to speak and quick to notice. He learned cord knots before letters, and the names of the stars before either.

When disputes came to the gathering ring, he would sit at the edge and, if asked, repeat exactly what each side had said, adding nothing of his own. By twelve, the elders were asking on purpose, mostly because he never seemed to want anyone to win.

III

Chapter IIIFirst wraps

At fourteen he took his first wraps, the pale undyed cloth of the dusk-keepers, wound chest first and hands last for the long work at the cord frames and the evening watch.

Dusk-keepers read the sky at the even hour and add each day to the spoken record, kind or unkind, without deciding what it meant. Rhada was suited to it from the first evening, and he still starts over if he winds his wraps in the wrong order.

IV

Chapter IVHalf and half

These days he splits his time the way Evergarden splits its days. Half goes to the clan, standing the evening watch, mending cloth and listening to arguments; the other half is his own, spent wandering the mesas and swapping stories with the traders who land a few times a year.

People who agree on very little both say they trust him, which is rarer than it should be. This file remains open.

08 · Habits & likesSmall Rituals

What he does with his hands and his evenings.

Habits

  • When listening Cheek against his hand, head tilted, silent until you have completely finished. If his hand is at his face, he is paying full attention.
  • When thinking Re-ties knots that do not need re-tying.
  • Every clear night Ties one knot in a long cord: a star calendar he has kept since he was small.
  • Favourite hour The even hour, just as the light tips into dark.

Likes

  • Dusk
  • River stones
  • Warm flatbread
  • The clack of a loom
  • Meals eaten slowly with other people

Not fond of

  • Being rushed
  • Loud certainty
  • Being told to pick a side before both have spoken
